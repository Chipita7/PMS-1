# 📘 User Guide - Project Assignments

## 🎯 How to Manage Your Project Assignments

---

## **Viewing Assignments**

### **Step 1: Navigate to Assigned Projects**
1. Click **"Projects"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Assigned to Me"**
3. You'll see a list of projects delegated to you

### **What You'll See:**
- **Project Name** - Title and description
- **Assigned To** - Your name (e.g., "Abiy")
- **Assigned By** - Who delegated it to you (e.g., "Sami")
- **Role** - Your role in the project:
  - 🟣 **Scrum Master** - Purple badge
  - 🔵 **Team Leader** - Blue badge
  - ⚪ **Member** - Gray badge
- **Due Date** - Project deadline
- **Status** - Current state (In Progress, To Do, etc.)
- **Progress** - Completion percentage
- **Attachments** - Files attached to the project
- **Actions** - Buttons to manage the assignment

---

## **Reviewing Project Details**

### **Step 2: Preview Before Deciding**
1. Click the **👁️ View** button next to any project
2. A dialog will open showing:
   - **Full project description**
   - **Who assigned it to you**
   - **Your role in the project**
   - **Due date and priority**
   - **List of all attachments**

### **From the Preview:**
- Click **"✓ Accept"** to approve the assignment
- Click **"✗ Reject"** to decline with a reason
- Click **"Close"** to go back without action

---

## **Accepting an Assignment**

### **Option 1: Accept from Preview (Recommended)**
1. Click **👁️ View** on the project
2. Review all details
3. Click **"✓ Accept"** button
4. Confirm in the approval dialog
5. **Result:** 
   - ✅ Project is approved
   - ✅ You're now assigned to the project
   - ✅ Project removed from pending list
   - ✅ Green success notification appears

### **Option 2: Quick Accept (Skip Preview)**
1. Click **"✓ Accept"** directly from the table
2. Confirm in the approval dialog
3. **Result:** Same as Option 1

---

## **Rejecting an Assignment**

### **Why Reject?**
- You're too busy with other projects
- The deadline is unrealistic
- You don't have the required skills
- You're on leave during the project period

### **How to Reject:**
1. Click **👁️ View** on the project (or **✗ Reject** directly)
2. Click **"✗ Reject"** button
3. **Important:** Enter a rejection reason (required)
   - Example: "I'm on leave during this period"
   - Example: "Already assigned to 3 critical projects"
   - Example: "Don't have expertise in this area"
4. Click **"Reject"** to confirm
5. **Result:**
   - ✅ Assignment declined
   - ✅ Your reason is recorded
   - ✅ Project removed from your list
   - ✅ Blue info notification appears

---

## **What Happens After Approval?**

### **Immediate Effects:**
1. ✅ Project **removed from "Assigned to Me" list**
2. ✅ Project **appears in "My Projects"** (as active)
3. ✅ You **can now work on the project**
4. ✅ You have access to:
   - Create tasks
   - Add milestones
   - Upload files
   - Collaborate with team
   - Track progress

### **Can You Change Your Mind?**
**Yes!** You can reject a project even after accepting it:
1. Go to **"My Projects"**
2. Find the project
3. Contact the project owner or use the reject feature
4. Provide a reason for the change

---

## **What Happens After Rejection?**

### **Immediate Effects:**
1. ✅ Assignment **declined**
2. ✅ Your reason **sent to project owner**
3. ✅ Project **removed from your list**
4. ✅ Project owner **notified** to reassign

### **Can You Change Your Mind?**
**Not automatically.** If you rejected by mistake:
1. Contact the project owner
2. Ask them to reassign the project to you
3. They'll send a new assignment
4. You'll see it in "Assigned to Me" again

## **Best Practices**

### **✅ DO:**
- **Review details** before accepting (click 👁️ View)
- **Check due date** - Can you meet it?
- **Review attachments** - Understand the requirements
- **Accept promptly** if you can do it
- **Provide clear reasons** when rejecting
- **Communicate** with project owner if unsure

### **❌ DON'T:**
- Don't accept without reviewing
- Don't reject without a reason
- Don't ignore pending assignments
- Don't accept if you can't deliver

## **Troubleshooting**

### **Q: I don't see any projects in "Assigned to Me"**
**A:** This means no one has assigned you to any projects yet, or all assignments have been processed.

### **Q: I accepted by mistake, can I undo?**
**A:** Yes! You can reject the project after accepting. Go to "My Projects", find it, and reject with a reason.

### **Q: Who can assign projects to me?**
**A:** Usually your manager, project owners, or anyone with permission to create projects and assign team members.

### **Q: What if the "Assigned By" shows a weird code?**
**A:** That's a temporary display issue. The system is trying to fetch the person's name. It should resolve shortly or show an 8-character code.

### **Q: Can I see rejected assignments?**
**A:** Currently, rejected assignments are removed from your view. Contact your manager if you need to review past rejections.

### **Q: Why did a project disappear after I accepted it?**
**A:** This is correct behavior! After accepting, it moves to "My Projects" (active work) and is removed from "Assigned to Me" (pending decisions).

## **Common Scenarios**

### **Scenario 1: Heavy Workload**
**Situation:** You're assigned to a project but already have 3 active projects.

**Action:**
1. Click 👁️ View
2. Check due date and complexity
3. If too much, click ✗ Reject
4. Reason: "Currently assigned to 3 critical projects with overlapping deadlines"
5. Alternative: Contact manager for priority guidance

---

### **Scenario 2: Skill Mismatch**
**Situation:** Assigned to a project requiring skills you don't have.

**Action:**
1. Click 👁️ View
2. Review requirements
3. Click ✗ Reject
4. Reason: "This project requires Python expertise, which is not my strong suit. Recommend assigning to [colleague name]"
5. Alternative: Accept if willing to learn (but communicate timeline)

---

### **Scenario 3: Vacation Conflict**
**Situation:** Project due date falls during your planned leave.

**Action:**
1. Click 👁️ View
2. Check due date
3. Click ✗ Reject
4. Reason: "On approved leave from [date] to [date], which conflicts with project timeline"
5. Alternative: Accept if you can complete before leave
